How each game mode works

Solo (play with bots)

Everything happens on your device. No game data is sent to anyone. If your phone is online, the ads library may request ads (section 7).

Local Group (hotspot or Wi-Fi)

When you host a game, your phone temporarily acts as a small game server and announces the game on your local network (game name, room code, number of players). Other devices on the same hotspot or Wi-Fi can see it and join. The host can see the nicknames of players who join. Game data travels directly between phones on that network and is not sent to our servers. This local traffic is not encrypted, so please use a hotspot protected by a password and play only with people you trust.

Online room

Online mode connects your device to a game server set in the app. The server passes positions, actions, votes and chat between the players in a room. It does not use a database of players, and room information disappears from the server when the room closes. Like any internet service, the server or its hosting provider may see your IP address and may keep short-lived technical logs for security and troubleshooting.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

MAYAVEX shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To do this, Google and its ad partners may collect and use information such as your Android advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This may be used to show ads that are more relevant to you (personalised ads), to limit how often you see an ad, to measure ad performance, and to detect fraud. Ads are shown only at natural breaks such as the end of a match or on menu screens, never in the middle of a Council.

Google’s use of this information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where the law requires your consent for personalised ads (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), ads are shown only in line with those requirements.

Your ad choices on Android: open Settings → Google → Ads (the path can differ slightly by phone). There you can reset your advertising ID, delete it, or opt out of ads personalisation. If you remove ads through a purchase (when available), no ads are shown.

In-app purchases

If we offer purchases (such as removing ads or cosmetic items), they are processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment details under its own terms and privacy policy. We receive only the information needed to confirm your purchase and unlock the item on your device.

Chat and player conduct

Council chat lets players type free text. Messages are visible to everyone in the room. Please do not share personal details such as your phone number or address, and be respectful. Harassment, hate speech, threats, sexual content and illegal content are not allowed. If you meet abusive behaviour, leave the room and report it to prismplustechnosoft2022@gmail.com with the room code and the nickname involved. We may remove players or content that break these rules.
