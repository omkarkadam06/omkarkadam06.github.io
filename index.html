The twist

It is MAYA HOUR

Early in every match, and again and again after that, the Veil falls for 15 seconds. Everyone becomes an identical glowing-eyed shadow, and names disappear.

  • A 6-second countdown warns you. The Council is sealed, so nobody can call for help.
  • A golden sanctuary ring around the Council Crystal keeps you safe from strikes.
  • Under the veil, nobody can tell who struck. Mirages wait for exactly this moment.
  • Echoes see through the veil. Mirages still see their partner.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is MAYAVEX free?

Yes, it is free to play. The game contains ads that appear only at natural breaks such as the end of a match, never during a Council.

Do I need internet to play?

No. Solo mode works fully offline, and Local Group works over a phone hotspot or the same Wi-Fi without internet. Online Room needs a connection.

How many players can join?

Matches support 4 to 10 players, with 1 or 2 Mirages depending on the room size.

How do I play with nearby friends?

One player turns on the phone hotspot, opens Local Group and taps Host. Friends connect to that hotspot, open Local Group and tap Join. The host starts the hunt once at least 3 players are in.

Which devices are supported?

MAYAVEX is an Android game, played in landscape mode on phones and tablets.

Is it suitable for children?

MAYAVEX is made for players aged 13 and over. It has stylised fantasy action with no blood. Online chat is visible to everyone in a room, so please be respectful.

How do I report a problem or abusive player?

Email us at prismplustechnosoft2022@gmail.com with the room code and the nickname involved.
